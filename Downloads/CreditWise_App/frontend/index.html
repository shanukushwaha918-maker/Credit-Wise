Reading the metrics

What each number measures

Precision
Of the applicants the model approved, the share that were truly approved.
Recall
Of the truly approved applicants, the share the model caught.
F1 score
The harmonic mean of precision and recall.
Accuracy
The share of all test applicants classified correctly.
